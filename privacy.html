Privacy Policy

CorePoker · Last updated August 15, 2026

CorePoker ("the app", "we", "us") is a home poker game manager that helps groups of friends track buy-ins, rebuys, expenses, and settle up after a live poker session. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

CorePoker collects only what's needed to run your account and your poker groups:

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. If you create an account with email and password instead, we collect the email address and password (handled securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password in plain text).
  • Game and group data you enter. This includes poker group names, player names within a group, buy-in and rebuy amounts, currency, expenses, and the settlement calculations the app generates from that data.
  • Device storage. The app also keeps a local copy of your data on your device (via browser local storage) so it keeps working offline and loads quickly.

We do not collect location data, contacts, camera/microphone access, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. CorePoker does not contain ads or any third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your poker groups and game history so they sync across your devices.
  • To let you share a group with other players via a share code or QR code, so co-hosts and players in the same group can see shared game and settlement data.
  • To calculate settlements (who owes whom) based on the buy-in and expense data you enter.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

How Your Information Is Shared

Within a poker group, your display name, email, and buy-in/settlement figures are visible to other members of that group — this is how the app is designed to work, since settling up requires everyone to see the same numbers. CorePoker does not process or transfer real money; it only calculates who owes whom. Any actual payment happens outside the app, between players, by whatever method they choose.

Where Your Data Is Stored

CorePoker uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) to store account and game data securely in the cloud, in addition to the local copy kept on your device for offline use. Firebase is operated by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below — we'll remove it from our systems within a reasonable time.

Children's Privacy

CorePoker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We rely on Firebase's built-in security infrastructure (encrypted connections, access-controlled data storage) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
